## 1. United States National Archives (NARA) — Record Group 59 (Department of State)

The primary repository for declassified U.S. diplomatic cables and embassy dispatches is the **National Archives at College Park, Maryland (Archives II)**.

### 1.1. NARA Access to Archival Databases (AAD)
* **Access URL:** `https://aad.archives.gov/aad/`
* **Target Collection:** *Central Foreign Policy Files (RG 59)*
  * Electronic Telegrams (1973–1979)
  * P-Reel & D-Reel Index (1973–1979)
* **Standard Diplomatic Subject TAGS to Query:**
  * `PGOV` — Internal Government Politics & Leadership
  * `PINR` — Political Intelligence & Biographic Reporting
  * `PHUM` — Human Rights Practices (crucial for sorcery/witchcraft killings)
  * `MARR` — Military & Defense Affairs (for UAP/UFO intercepts)
  * `OSCI` — Science & Technology (psychotronics and microwave signals)

### 1.2. Key NARA Search Formulations
1. **Robert Toth / Soviet Parapsychology Interrogation:**
   * Query: `TOTH, ROBERT` AND `PARAPSYCHOLOGY` OR `PETUKHOV`
   * Target Telegrams: `1977MOSCOW08541` through `1977MOSCOW08720`
2. **Sawito Affair & Javanese Mystical Mandate:**
   * Query: `SAWITO` AND `JAKARTA`
   * Target Telegrams: `1976JAKART11840` through `1976JAKART12500`
3. **Legalization of Aliran Kepercayaan (GBHN 1978):**
   * Query: `KEPERCAYAAN` OR `KEBATINAN` AND `JAKARTA`
   * Target Telegrams: `1978JAKART03912` through `1978JAKART04100`

## 3. WikiLeaks Public Library of US Diplomacy (PlusD / Cablegate)

WikiLeaks hosts the largest public, fully searchable text database of State Department telegrams spanning 1966 to 2010.

* **Access URL:** `https://wikileaks.org/plusd/`
* **Advanced Query Operators:**
  * `origin:Jakarta` — Limits results to AMEMBASSY Jakarta.
  * `concept:"POLITICAL LEADERS"` — Filters for leadership personality profiling.

1. **Burmese Astrological Demonetization:**
   * Query: `origin:Rangoon "astrology" AND "kyats"`

   * Period: August 1998 – December 1998

   * Target Cables: AMEMBASSY Dar es Salaam (2008–2010 series)

## 4. The National Security Archive (George Washington University)

The National Security Archive at GWU is the world's leading independent non-governmental research institute utilizing FOIA to declassify U.S. government documents.

* **Access URL:** `https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/`
* **Key Document Collections:**
  * **Indonesia and East Timor Documentation Project:** Houses hundreds of declassified White House, State Department, and CIA cables on Sukarno, Soeharto, G30S, and the 1974–1975 East Timor annexation discussions.
  * **The Moscow Signal Declassified:** Comprehensive compilation of Henry Kissinger's secret memoranda of conversation (MemCons) with Soviet Ambassador Anatoly Dobrynin regarding the embassy microwave radiation.
  * **U.S. Government Declassified UFO Documents:** The complete compilation of military, CIA, and State Department files on anomalous aerial encounters.

## 5. Indonesian State Repositories: Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia (ANRI) & Official Inquiries

For researchers investigating the domestic Indonesian side of state mysticism and parapsychic intelligence:

### 5.1. Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia (ANRI)
* **Location:** Jl. Ampera Raya No. 7, Cilandak, Jakarta Selatan
* **Target Fond / Inventaris Arsip:**
  * **Inventaris Arsip Kepresidenan Sukarno (1945–1967):** Contains correspondence, ceremonial protocols, and decrees concerning state symbols, pusaka, and the reservation of Room 308 at Samudera Beach Hotel.
  * **Inventaris Arsip Sekretariat Kabinet / Sekretariat Negara RI Era Orde Baru:** Documents the formal administrative establishment of Aspri (Asisten Pribadi Presiden) under Mayjen Sujono Humardani and Letjen Ali Murtopo.
  * **Berkas Sidang Umum MPR 1978:** The verbatim legislative transcripts (*risalah sidang*) detailing the constitutional debate and the PPP walkout over *Aliran Kepercayaan*.

### 5.2. Kejaksaan Agung RI — Bakorpakem Files
* **Entity:** *Badan Koordinasi Pengawasan Aliran Kepercayaan dan Keagamaan dalam Masyarakat (Bakorpakem)*.
* **Archival Holdings:** Case files, surveillance dossiers, and prohibitory decrees issued against heterodox spiritual groups, charismatic sects, and controversial mysticism movements from 1952 to the present.

### 5.3. Komnas HAM (National Commission on Human Rights)
* **Target Dossier:** *Laporan Tim Penyelidikan Peristiwa Pembunuhan Dukun Santet di Banyuwangi dan Daerah Lainnya Tahun 1998*.
* **Significance:** The most exhaustive forensic and sociological investigation into the 1998 "ninja" killings, detailing victim profiles, autopsy findings, and field evidence demonstrating coordinated military/intelligence psychological operations.
