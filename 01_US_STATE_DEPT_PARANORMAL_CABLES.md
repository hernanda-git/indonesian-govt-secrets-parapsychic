## 1. Executive Summary & Nature of Diplomatic Reporting

The United States Department of State (DOS) and its diplomatic missions abroad (Embassies, Consulates, and Missions to International Organizations) are not scientific research agencies. However, throughout modern history, diplomatic cables (*telegrams*), airgrams, intelligence memoranda, and consular dispatches have systematically recorded, analyzed, and intervened in phenomena falling into the realm of the **spiritual, supernatural, parapsychic, occult, and unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP/UFO)**.

Declassified records demonstrate that the State Department encounters these phenomena through five primary national security and foreign policy vectors:
1. **Host-Nation Political Intelligence:** Foreign heads of state and military commanders making geopolitical, economic, or military decisions based on astrologers, shamans, mystics, or occult rituals.
2. **Psychological & Parapsychological Warfare:** Cold War intelligence battles regarding Soviet "psychotronics", extrasensory perception (ESP), remote viewing, and electromagnetic mind/behavioral control.
3. **Tactical Military Incidents & Airspace Violations:** Direct encounters by host-nation militaries with anomalous aerial phenomena reported through Defense Attaché Offices (DAO) in embassies.
4. **Crisis Management & Hostage Tracking:** The operational deployment of parapsychic intelligence (remote viewing) to map seized embassy compounds and locate detained American diplomats.
5. **Human Rights & Consular Affairs:** State-sanctioned witch hunts, ritualistic murders, and apocalyptic cults operating in foreign jurisdictions.

## 2. Comprehensive Inventory: UAP & Unidentified Aerial Phenomena

The State Department's diplomatic communications network (the "Telegram" system) frequently served as the transmission pipeline for foreign military and civil observations of unidentified aerial phenomena.

### 2.1. The 1976 Tehran UFO Incident (AMEMBASSY Tehran to SECSTATE / DIA / JCS)
* **Date of Event:** September 19, 1976
* **Originating Post:** Defense Attaché Office (DAO), American Embassy Tehran, Iran
* **Recipient Offices:** Secretary of State (SECSTATE Washington DC), Joint Chiefs of Staff (JCS), Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA), Central Intelligence Agency (CIA), White House
* **Document Identifier:** Cable IR 6 846 0036 76 / DIA Evaluation Report
* **Classification:** Secret (Declassified under FOIA)
* **Incident Summary:**
  * At 12:30 AM, civilian radar and visual observers in Tehran spotted a brilliant, pulsating aerial object over the Shemiran district.
  * Imperial Iranian Air Force (IIAF) scrambled an F-4 Phantom II from Shahrokhi AFB. At 25 nautical miles from the object, all radar and UHF communications failed simultaneously. The pilot broke off, and systems restored immediately.
  * A second F-4 piloted by Lt. Jafari achieved a radar lock on the object at 27 nautical miles. The object moved at high speed (estimated supersonic) and released a smaller, brilliant orb that flew directly toward the F-4.
  * The pilot attempted to fire an AIM-9 Sidewinder missile; instantly, the weapons control panel went dead, and communications were completely lost. The pilot executed a high-G evasive dive.
  * The primary object then released a second subsidiary craft that landed smoothly in the desert south of Tehran, illuminating the terrain.
* **Diplomatic Assessment:** The embassy cable concluded with a formal evaluation rating the incident as an *"outstanding report"* characterized by high reliability: multiple credible witnesses, radar correlation from ground and air, simultaneous instrument failure across two separate military aircraft, and physiological sensations reported by the flight crews.

### 2.2. The United Nations UFO & Parapsychology Initiative (1977–1978)
* **Key Actors:** Sir Eric Matthew Gairy (Prime Minister of Grenada), Cyrus Vance (U.S. Secretary of State), U.S. Mission to the United Nations (USUN New York)
* **Document Identifiers:** State Department Telegrams 1977-STATE-285642, 1978-USUN-04821, 1978-STATE-291845
* **Classification:** Confidential / Limited Official Use (Declassified)
* **Diplomatic Context:**
  * Prime Minister Eric Gairy of Grenada formally petitioned the UN General Assembly to establish a dedicated UN agency or department for the investigation of Unidentified Flying Objects, extraterrestrial intelligence, and psychic/metaphysical phenomena.
  * In November 1978, Gairy brought a delegation including astronaut Gordon Cooper, astronomer Dr. J. Allen Hynek, and computer scientist/ufologist Jacques Vallée to testify before the UN Special Political Committee.
* **State Department Strategy (Documented in Cables):**
  * State Department cables reveal deep American concern that a formal UN UFO/parapsychology resolution would establish an expensive, unscientific bureaucracy and create diplomatic embarrassment.
  * Secretary of State Cyrus Vance issued specific instructions to USUN diplomats: do not publicly ridicule Gairy or alienate Grenada, but quietly lobby other delegations (notably the UK, West Germany, and Commonwealth states) to dilute the resolution.
  * The diplomatic maneuvering successfully amended the proposal from creating a UN UFO Agency into a toothless recommendation that member states simply share academic research through existing UN organs. (Gairy was subsequently overthrown in a coup in March 1979 while abroad).

## 3. Cold War Parapsychology, Remote Viewing, & Psychotronics

### 3.1. Project GRILL FLAME / STARGATE & The Tehran Embassy Hostages (1979–1981)
* **Operating Agencies:** DIA, U.S. Army Intelligence and Security Command (INSCOM), Central Intelligence Agency (CIA), State Department Iran Task Force
* **Archival Repository:** CIA-RDP96-00788R / CREST Declassified Records
* **Operational Scope:**
  * When 52 American diplomats were taken hostage at the U.S. Embassy in Tehran on November 4, 1979, conventional intelligence assets inside Iran were almost completely severed.
  * The Pentagon and the National Security Council activated **Project GRILL FLAME** at Fort Meade, Maryland. Parapsychic "remote viewers" (including Joseph McMoneagle, Ingo Swann, and Hartmut Mueller) were tasked with conducting psychic reconnaissance directly against the sovereign grounds of the U.S. Embassy compound.
* **Documented Remote Viewing Sessions:**
  * Over 134 operational remote viewing sessions were executed between November 1979 and January 1981 specifically targeting the embassy.
  * *Targeting Bruce Laingen (Chargé d'Affaires):* Remote viewers correctly identified that Laingen and two aides (Victor Tomseth and Michael Howland) were not held at the embassy compound itself, but were detained separately under armed guard at the Iranian Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MFA).
  * *Compound Floor Plans:* Viewers sketched internal layouts of the Ambassador's Residence, Chancery basement, and Mushroom Building, attempting to pinpoint the movement of hostages between rooms and identifying armed sentry posts.
  * *Rescue Mission Support:* Data generated by GRILL FLAME was fed to the Joint Special Operations Task Force (JSOTF) planning *Operation Eagle Claw* (the April 1980 Tabas desert rescue attempt). Briefings and correlation memos were transmitted to the Special Coordination Committee (SCC) and the State Department's Iran Working Group.

### 3.2. The Robert Toth Affair & Soviet Psychotronics (Embassy Moscow, 1977)
* **Date:** June 11–18, 1977
* **Originating Post:** U.S. Embassy Moscow
* **Key Persons:** Robert C. Toth (Journalist, *Los Angeles Times*), Valery G. Petukhov (Soviet Biophysicist), Ambassador Malcolm Toon
* **Archival Identifier:** NARA Record Group 59, Central Foreign Policy Files, 1977-MOSCOW-08541 to 08720
* **Incident Analysis:**
  * On June 11, 1977, Toth was seized on a Moscow street by KGB agents immediately after receiving a manuscript from Petukhov titled *The Bio-Physical Nature of Telepathic Communications*.
  * Toth was subjected to days of intensive interrogation at Lefortovo Prison. The KGB interrogators explicitly stated that **parapsychology, telepathy, and "mitogenic radiation" were classified state secrets** in the USSR, as the Soviet Academy of Sciences was researching their use for submarine communications and psycho-energetic warfare.
  * Declassified telegrams reveal that U.S. Embassy Moscow treated the incident as a major diplomatic clash. Ambassador Toon met directly with Soviet Deputy Foreign Minister Georgy Kornienko, issuing formal diplomatic demarches. The cables highlight the surreal intersection where the superpowers clashed diplomatically over the alleged espionage of "psychic energy particles".

### 3.3. The Moscow Microwave Signal & Project Pandora (1953–1976)
* **Location:** U.S. Embassy Moscow (Chaikovsky Street)
* **Investigating Bodies:** Department of State Medical Division, DARPA, CIA Office of Scientific Intelligence (OSI)
* **Declassified Project Code:** Project Pandora / Project TUMS / Project MUTE
* **Core Issue:**
  * From 1953 onward, Western sensors detected a continuous, focused microwave beam (2.5 to 4.0 GHz) directed at the upper floors of the American Embassy in Moscow from an adjacent Soviet apartment complex.
  * For years, State Department leadership and intelligence analysts wrestled with the objective: was this merely remote electronic activation of passive audio listening bugs (such as Léon Theremin's "Thing"), or was it an offensive application of **Soviet psychotronics**?
  * Declassified memos from DARPA's *Project Pandora* (initiated under State Department agreement) show that scientists conducted experiments on primates to determine whether the microwave radiation was intended to induce neurological damage, behavioral modification, chronic depression, disorientation, or cancer among American diplomatic personnel.
  * When Ambassador Walter Stoessel threatened to resign in 1975 unless staff were informed of health hazards, Secretary of State Henry Kissinger engaged in secret negotiations with Soviet Ambassador Anatoly Dobrynin to halt the radiation beams.

## 5. Occult, Witchcraft, & Mysticism in Diplomatic Statecraft

Foreign service officers routinely dispatch comprehensive assessments of foreign leaders whose governance, military strategy, or succession plans are driven by spiritual or occult practices.

### 5.1. Haiti: François "Papa Doc" Duvalier & State Vodou (Embassy Port-au-Prince)
* **Cables:** Declassified 1960s–1970s Port-au-Prince dispatches (RG 59).
* **Diplomatic Reporting:**
  * Embassy political officers documented how President François Duvalier systematically co-opted Haitian Vodou (*Vaudou*) as an instrument of totalitarian control.
  * Duvalier deliberately styled his physical appearance—black suit, narrow tie, slow nasal cadence, and dark thick glasses—after **Baron Samedi**, the Vodou *loa* (spirit) of death and cemeteries.
  * Cables described how the rural secret police and militia, the *Tonton Macoute* (named after the folklore child-eating bogeyman *Tonton Macoute*), were recruited from local *houngans* (Vodou priests).
  * State Department memos analyzed how the population's belief in Duvalier's supernatural invulnerability effectively paralyzed internal political opposition and complicated diplomatic pressure from the Kennedy and Johnson administrations.

### 5.2. Burma (Myanmar): Astrological Statecraft & Demonetization (Embassy Rangoon)
* **Cables:** 1980s Rangoon political reporting; Cable 1987-RANGOON-04912.
* **Diplomatic Reporting:**
  * Embassy Rangoon dispatched detailed accounts of military dictator General Ne Win's pathological reliance on *yadaya* (Burmese astrological/magical rituals designed to ward off misfortune).
  * **The 1987 Currency Catastrophe:** On September 5, 1987, Ne Win abruptly issued an executive decree demonetizing Burma's 25, 35, and 75-kyat banknotes without compensation or warning, wiping out approximately 75% of the nation's circulating currency. He replaced them exclusively with 45 and 90-kyat banknotes.
  * Embassy cables explained the baffling monetary policy: Ne Win's personal astrologer had calculated that the number **9** was the general's mystical life-number. The new denominations were chosen because 45 (4+5=9) and 90 (9+0=9) were divisible by 9, intended to grant him spiritual longevity and political survival.
  * The embassy correctly predicted that this superstition-driven economic collapse would trigger catastrophic civil unrest, which culminated in the historic **8888 Uprising** in August 1988.

### 5.3. Uganda: Idi Amin's Prophetic Dreams & Sorcery (Embassy Kampala)
* **Cables:** Declassified 1971–1979 Kampala telegrams (RG 59).
* **Diplomatic Reporting:**
  * Diplomatic cables from Kampala described General Idi Amin Dada's reliance on prophetic dreams, ancestral divination, and consultations with traditional sorcerers (*witch doctors*) from the Kakwa ethnic group.
  * Amin frequently justified major geopolitical and human rights decisions—including the sudden expulsion of 80,000 Ugandan Asians in August 1972 and dramatic troop deployments—by claiming God or ancestral spirits had instructed him during nocturnal visions.

### 5.4. Sri Lanka: Astrologically Timed Elections (Embassy Colombo)
* **Cables:** Colombo political dispatches (1970s–2000s; Cablegate 07COLOMBO124).
* **Diplomatic Reporting:**
  * Embassy Colombo cables consistently reported on the institutional role of the *Nekath* (state auspicious times calculated by royal/presidential astrologers).
  * Dispatches noted that major Sri Lankan presidents (including Ranasinghe Premadasa and Mahinda Rajapaksa) refused to sign treaties, dissolve parliament, or announce election dates without exact to-the-minute astrological timing deemed spiritually favorable, an essential data point for Western diplomats scheduling bilateral summits.

## 6. Apocalyptic Cults, Sects, & Spiritual Mass Casualties

### 6.1. The Jonestown Massacre / Peoples Temple (Embassy Georgetown, Guyana, 1974–1978)
* **Key Documents:** Over 250 declassified telegrams between AMEMBASSY Georgetown and the Department of State (documented under the Ryan Congressional Investigation and FOIA Case F-2007-00914).
* **Chronicle:**
  * From 1974 onward, cables tracked the migration of Jim Jones's California-based "Peoples Temple" to the remote jungle commune of Jonestown in Guyana.
  * Consular cables documented visits by consular officers to check on welfare, responding to American family petitions alleging that Jones was running an armed, mind-controlling apocalyptic cult using staged "faith healings" and psychological torture.
  * The cables culminate in the catastrophic events of November 17–18, 1978: the ambush and murder of U.S. Congressman Leo Ryan, three journalists, and a defecting temple member on the Port Kaituma airstrip, followed by the forced cyanide poisoning and suicide of 918 followers in Jonestown.
  * The post-mortem State Department reviews completely overhauled consular crisis procedures regarding American citizens trapped in foreign religious communes.

* **Cables:** 1995 Tokyo dispatches regarding Shoko Asahara and Aum Shinrikyo.
* **Diplomatic Reporting:**
  * Embassy reporting tracked the evolution of Aum Shinrikyo from a fringe apocalyptic Buddhist/Hindu/Nostradamus spiritual movement into an armed terrorist organization producing chemical (sarin, VX) and biological (anthrax, botulinum) weapons.

## 7. Official Human Rights Reporting: Witchcraft Accusations & Ritual Killings

Through the *Country Reports on Human Rights Practices* published annually by the State Department under congressional mandate (22 U.S.C. § 2151n and § 2304), U.S. Embassies monitor state and non-state violence rooted in supernatural beliefs:

### 7.1. Papua New Guinea: *Sanguma* (Sorcery Accusations)
* **Embassy Monitoring:** Embassy Port Moresby cables and annual human rights submissions track violence related to sorcery accusations (*sanguma*).
* **Documented Abuses:** Elderly or marginalized women accused of using black magic to cause sudden deaths or illness are subjected to public torture, burning, and lynching.
* **Diplomatic Action:** State Department reports actively documented and supported the PNG Parliament's 2013 repeal of the British colonial *Sorcery Act 1971*, which had previously recognized "provocation by sorcery" as a valid legal defense for assault or murder.

### 7.2. Tanzania, Malawi, & Mozambique: Ritual Murders of Albinos
* **Embassy Monitoring:** AMEMBASSY Dar es Salaam cables (e.g., 2008–2015).
* **Documented Abuses:** Systematic tracking of the targeted murder and dismemberment of persons with albinism (*albinos*). The cables describe how corrupt local sorcerers (*witch doctors*) fabricate talismans and potions containing albino bones, hair, and limbs, selling them to illegal miners and fishermen under the superstitious belief that they generate instant wealth.
* **U.S. Interventions:** Embassies supported local NGOs, pushed host governments for strict capital murder prosecutions of witch doctors, and provided consular and refugee support to targeted victims.

### 7.3. Ghana & Burkina Faso: "Witch Camps"
* **Embassy Monitoring:** Embassy Accra reporting on the infamous *Gnani* and *Gambaga* "witch camps" in Northern Ghana.
* **Documented Abuses:** Thousands of women banished by village chiefs on allegations of witchcraft, forced to live in squalid, segregated settlements without legal recourse.

## 8. Haunted Embassies & Ghost Lore in Official Foreign Service History

Beyond classified cables, the **Association for Diplomatic Studies and Training (ADST)** and the State Department's Foreign Affairs Oral History Collection preserve firsthand accounts from Ambassadors and Foreign Service Officers regarding paranormal occurrences inside official diplomatic properties:

### 8.1. Spaso House (U.S. Ambassador’s Residence, Moscow)
* **Historical Significance:** A 1914 neo-classical mansion leased by the U.S. government since diplomatic relations were established with the USSR in 1933. Venue for Ambassador William Bullitt's legendary 1935 Spring Ball, which inspired Mikhail Bulgakov's depiction of the "Midnight Spring Ball of the Full Moon" (Satan's Ball) in *The Master and Margarita*.
* **Documented Lore:** Generations of American ambassadors, resident staff, and Marine Security Guards (MSG) have reported unexplained phenomena: sudden inexplicable temperature drops in the upper ballroom, ghostly piano melodies resonating in locked, unoccupied drawing rooms, and spectral figures observed moving down the central marble staircase late at night.

### 8.2. Schoenborn Palace (U.S. Embassy, Prague)
* **Historical Significance:** A sprawling 17th-century Baroque palace in the Malá Strana district featuring over 100 rooms, extensive terraced gardens, and centuries of Bohemian nobility history.
* **Documented Lore:** Diplomatic officers and security details frequently recount eerie occurrences in the unoccupied upper chambers and historic attic suites (once briefly rented by Franz Kafka in 1917). Reports include disembodied footsteps pacing the parquet hallways, heavy antique doors latching and unlocking without human presence, and persistent shadows observed along the lower garden colonnades.
